INTRODUCTION

🌱 What Are Plant Diseases?

Plant diseases are abnormal conditions that affect the normal growth, development and functioning of plants. A healthy plant normally grows, produces flowers, fruits and seeds. However, when a plant is affected by a disease, its normal activities are disturbed. The disease may damage different parts of the plant, including roots, stems, leaves, flowers and fruits.

Plant diseases are mainly caused by harmful microorganisms such as fungi, bacteria and viruses. They can also develop due to unfavourable environmental conditions such as extreme temperatures, lack of nutrients, excess water and drought. Some diseases spread through soil, seeds, insects, water, wind and infected plant materials.

Common symptoms include yellow leaves, brown spots, wilting, leaf curling, rotting, unusual growth and premature falling of leaves. These symptoms can reduce the productivity and quality of crops.

🌾 Importance of Identifying Plant Diseases

Identifying plant diseases at an early stage is very important in agriculture. When farmers recognize disease symptoms early, they can take suitable preventive and control measures before the disease spreads to other plants.

Early identification reduces crop losses, improves agricultural product quality, saves farming expenses and protects farmers' income. It also helps reduce unnecessary pesticide use and supports environmentally friendly farming.

🌿 Why Should We Protect Crops?

Agriculture is an important source of food and livelihood for millions of people. Protecting crops from diseases helps maintain food production, improve farmers' economic conditions and support food security. Healthy crops also contribute to a healthy environment and sustainable agriculture.

🔬 Common Causes of Plant Diseases

🍄 Fungi

Cause rust, powdery mildew, leaf spots, blights and root rot.

🦠 Bacteria

Cause bacterial spots, bacterial wilt, soft rot and tissue damage.

🌿 Viruses

Cause mosaic patterns, yellowing, leaf curling and stunted growth.

☀️ Environment

Drought, excess moisture, poor drainage, nutrient deficiency and extreme temperatures damage plants.

🌻 Prevention and Control

1. Use healthy and disease-free seeds.
2. Maintain proper irrigation and drainage.
3. Remove and safely dispose of infected plant parts.
4. Maintain cleanliness in agricultural fields.
5. Follow crop rotation practices.
6. Use disease-resistant plant varieties.
7. Control insect pests that spread plant viruses.
8. Consult agricultural experts for correct treatment.

🌱 Objective of Our Community Service Project

The main objective of our Community Service Project is to create awareness among farmers and local communities about different plant diseases, their symptoms, causes, prevention and management. Through field visits, observations and interaction with farmers, we aim to understand the challenges faced in crop protection and share useful information about maintaining healthy plants.
